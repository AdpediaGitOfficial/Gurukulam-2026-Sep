Two design calls I made, and why

The course bars are one colour, not four. Every bar measures the same thing — how much of that course has been delivered. Four hues would encode position in the list, so re-sorting or filtering repaints them and the colour means nothing. Magnitude gets one hue; colour is reserved for retail-against-college, where it carries a real distinction.

A missing measurement shows as "—", never as 0%. A drop-out rate of 0.0% reads as "nobody drops out". "—" reads as "nobody is counting", which is true and is the thing worth fixing.

What I'd recommend

Ship Option A now — six of the ten data points are live and need no schema change. Add the roster's complete/exit verb in the same pass and completion and drop-out light up a day later, taking it to eight. Punctuality and rating wait for the trainer and student portals, which is where the people who actually know those facts will be.
